The Tree Metaphor

大树隐喻：言语的阈值启动与递归生长

A compact theoretical metaphor for how speech launches from locally sufficient organization and grows through incremental and recursive network formation.

The tree metaphor represents speech production as open-ended generation: an utterance is not globally completed before speech begins, but launches when a local structure crosses a threshold under the constraint of an overarching intention, and then develops through incrementality and recursion.

Macroplanning: the branch network

Through macroplanning, the overarching intention diffuses into a Subgoal Network. Linearization establishes relations and an order of advance among subgoals, forming the macroscopic trunk-and-branch organization of discourse growth. When processing enters the current subgoal, functional orientation specifies the local direction of growth, conceptual focus becomes the organizing center, and relevant conceptual nodes form a Local Conceptual Network.

Microplanning: the local bud

The local conceptual network is analogous to a bud at a node. Although not yet extended into a branch, a bud already possesses a growth center, an internal configuration, an initial axis, and a connection to the parent branch. Correspondingly, a microplan is not yet a complete utterance, but it has organized the conceptual elements required around the current focus, established a minimal relational configuration, and retained a functional connection to the current subgoal and the discourse already produced.

Threshold: the onset of growth

The threshold marks the critical transition from potential organization to linguistic realization. When the minimal relational configuration centered on the conceptual focus acquires the capacity for linguistic encoding, the bud begins to sprout and the local structure enters formulation. The threshold is therefore not the completion of planning, but the moment at which a local network with a minimally sufficient generative configuration acquires the capacity for actual growth.

Incrementality and recursion

Formulation initiates the extension of the branch. Incrementality incorporates new conceptual nodes into the current local network; recursion allows a newly incorporated node to become a new organizing center and trigger another local network. Each newly formed structure is both the outcome of current processing and a condition for subsequent processing.

Macroplanning and microplanning thus instantiate the same network-generating logic at different scales. Macroplanning generates and develops the subgoal network; microplanning generates and develops the local conceptual network. Both sustain speech through node formation, threshold crossing, incremental expansion, and recursive reorganization.

Macroplanning generates the branch network; microplanning forms the local bud; the threshold initiates sprouting; incrementality drives extension; recursion produces new buds.

大树隐喻

The Tree Metaphor

言语的阈值启动与递归生长

大树隐喻表征言语产出的开放性生成：话语并非在开口之前整体完成，而是在总意图约束下，由局部结构越过阈值后启动，并通过增量与递归逐步形成。

宏计划：枝干网络

总意图经宏计划扩散为 Subgoal Network；线性化使子目标获得可推进的关系与次序，构成话语生长的宏观枝干结构。当前子目标进入微计划后，功能定向规定局部生长方向，概念焦点成为组织中心，相关概念节点围绕其形成 Local Conceptual Network。

微计划：局部芽体

局部概念网络如同节点处尚未萌发的芽。芽虽未展开为枝条，却已具有生长中心、内部构型、初始生长轴及其与母枝的连接；相应地，微计划虽未形成完整话语，却已围绕当前概念焦点组织必要概念成分，建立最低关系配置，并与当前子目标及既有话语保持功能联系。

阈值：现实生长的启动

阈值标记局部网络由潜在组织转入语言化的临界状态。当以概念焦点为中心的最低关系配置获得语言化能力，芽便开始萌发，局部结构进入语言编码。阈值因此不是计划完成，而是一个已经形成最低生成构型的局部网络获得现实生长能力。

增量与递归

语言编码使枝条开始延伸；增量使新的概念节点持续进入当前局部网络；递归使新节点成为新的组织中心，触发下一轮局部网络生成。新形成的结构既是当前加工的结果，也是后续加工的条件。

宏计划与微计划由此表现为同一网络生成逻辑在不同尺度上的运行：宏计划生成并展开子目标网络，微计划生成并展开局部概念网络；二者都通过节点形成、阈值越过、增量扩展与递归再组织实现持续生长。

宏计划生成枝干网络，微计划形成局部芽体，阈值启动萌发，增量推动延伸，递归产生新芽。
