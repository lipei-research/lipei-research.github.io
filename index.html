The Central Contribution
Intellectual Foundations

The Operable Mechanism takes speech-production theory as its overarching architecture and brings conceptual structure, cognitive grammar, systemic functional linguistics, working memory, and cognitive fluency into a unified account that renders the otherwise hidden preverbal processes preceding speech observable, explicable, and operable.

Speech Production · Conceptualization

Levelt’s architecture of speech production is the principal foundation of the operable mechanism. English Speaking Lab
Research-Based Spoken English Education

English Speaking Lab

英语口语研究室

The Lab brings together four interconnected modules—American Pronunciation, American Connected Speech, a Sentence-Construction Framework, and a Fluency Mechanism—to form a systematic pathway from accurate pronunciation and natural speech flow to clear sentence construction and fluent, sustained production.

01 · American PronunciationUsing KK phonetic notation and articulatory settings as its foundation, this module develops accurate, stable, and self-monitored American English pronunciation through phonemic contrast, diagnostic listening, and guided self-correction.
02 · American Connected SpeechThis module systematically trains linking, unreleased stops, weak forms, stress, rhythm, and intonation to develop a more natural, authentic, and connected flow of speech.
03 · Sentence-Construction FrameworkDrawing on traditional grammar, systemic functional linguistics, philosophy of language, and cognitive linguistics, this module develops an explanatory framework for how English sentences are generated, expanded, and organized.
04 · Fluency MechanismCentered on the Speech Launch Threshold, this module makes otherwise opaque preverbal processing explicit and organizes it into operable steps, helping learners initiate and sustain more fluent L2 speech under real-time pressure.
Research Journey
2008–2010

The Initial Problem: From Accurate Form to Generative Speech

Intensive work on American pronunciation achieved a high degree of articulatory automaticity, yet traditional grammar study did not yield equally flexible sentence generation. This contrast initiated a long-term inquiry into how linguistic knowledge becomes usable in real time.

2010–2013

Language, Mind, and the Architecture of Production

Study in linguistic philosophy and multiple grammatical traditions led to an integrated personal syntax framework. Encountering Levelt’s Speaking: From Intention to Articulation, followed by M.A. training in English Interpreting at Nankai University, shifted the question from linguistic description to the cognitive organization of speech.

2014–2016

Systematizing Knowledge for Teaching

University teaching transformed earlier work on pronunciation, sound change, grammar, and spoken performance into explicit instructional systems. National first prizes in teaching and micro-lecture competitions confirmed the pedagogical value of making complex linguistic knowledge visible and operable.

2017–2020

The Fluency Impasse and the Attentional Turn

Persistent difficulty in sustaining spontaneous speech, despite substantial linguistic knowledge, exposed a gap between knowledge availability and online usability. The central problem was gradually redefined in terms of limited attention, monitoring load, and real-time processing pressure.

2021

Buying Processing Time

Engagement with Segalowitz’s cognitive account of second-language fluency led to a key empirical question: whether slowing speech and extending pauses could release attentional resources and provide more time for online language processing.

2022–2023

From Fluency Support to an Operable Mechanism

A Tianjin Municipal Education Commission project extended the inquiry beyond temporal strategies toward the mechanism operating within the time they provide. Research on macroplan linearization examined how complex intentions can be decomposed into ordered subgoals before formulation.

2023–2024

Entering Cognitive Science and Reconstructing the Conceptualizer

Work across cognitive science, conceptual semantics, cognitive grammar, working memory, and L2 production models supported a return to Levelt’s Conceptualizer. The emerging model began to explain how declarative knowledge could be selected, organized, and converted into a preverbal message online.

2024

Model Construction and AI-Supported Application

A China Foreign Language Education Fund project advanced formal model construction, while research on ChatGPT voice interaction explored how an operable production mechanism could inform spoken-language practice, feedback, and instructional design.

2025

Research–Teaching Integration

Publication of the operable online-production mechanism and major teaching awards marked the convergence of psycholinguistic modeling, visual explanation, and classroom application into a coherent research-led pedagogy.

2026

The Speech Launch Threshold

The conference model specifies a minimum launchable state—functional orientation, conceptual focus, and minimal relational configuration—followed by incremental formulation, recursive re-triggering, and attentional support under real-time pressure.
