Research Note

Intellectual Foundations

Taking psycholinguistic speech-production theory as its overarching architecture, the Operable Mechanism moves inside the Conceptualizer and integrates working memory, conceptual structure, cognitive grammar, systemic functional linguistics, and cognitive fluency to explain how communicative intention is preverbally organized through the joint operation of mental representation and attentional control, and transformed into speech that can be smoothly initiated and continuously advanced.

Levelt’s architecture of speech production is the principal foundation of the operable mechanism. Academic Architecture
01

Research

Investigates how declarative knowledge is organized online—from communicative intention through macroplanning, linearization, microplanning, and the Speech Launch Threshold to incremental formulation—so that L2 speakers can convert available knowledge into fluent speech under real-time pressure.

02

Publications

Builds the mechanism’s empirical and theoretical foundation through studies of speech-rate reduction and pause extension, macroplan linearization, AI-mediated spoken interaction, and the operable online production mechanism itself.

03

Projects

Advances a cumulative program—from an institutional study of processing-time support, through a Tianjin Municipal Education Commission project on operable online production, to a China Foreign Language Education Fund project on model construction—that progressively tests and formalizes the mechanism.

04

Talks

Presents the model as a coherent account of online L2 speech production, culminating in the 2026 International Conference on Biolinguistics and Language Acquisition, where the Speech Launch Threshold connects conceptual preparation with articulation onset.

05

Awards

Holds four individual distinctions: first prize in the 2015 FLTRP “Star Teacher” National Final; first prize in the 2016 China Foreign Language Micro-Lecture National Final; the 2025 national grand prize in the National College English Teachers’ Basic Teaching Skills Competition; and first prize in the 2025 SFLEP National Foreign Language Teaching Contest, Tianjin Final. Each award arose from translating research on pronunciation, syntax, and online speech production into teachable and operable instructional systems, tracing a sustained path from research to pedagogy.

English Speaking Lab
Research-Based Spoken English Education

English Speaking Lab

英语口语研究室

The Lab brings together four interconnected modules—American Pronunciation, American Connected Speech, a Sentence-Construction Framework, and a Fluency Mechanism—to form a systematic pathway from accurate pronunciation and natural speech flow to clear sentence construction and fluent, sustained production.

01 · American PronunciationUsing KK phonetic notation and articulatory settings as its foundation, this module develops accurate, stable, and self-monitored American English pronunciation through phonemic contrast, diagnostic listening, and guided self-correction.
02 · American Connected SpeechThis module systematically trains linking, unreleased stops, weak forms, stress, rhythm, and intonation to develop a more natural, authentic, and connected flow of speech.
03 · Sentence-Construction FrameworkDrawing on traditional grammar, systemic functional linguistics, philosophy of language, and cognitive linguistics, this module develops an explanatory framework for how English sentences are generated, expanded, and organized.
04 · Fluency MechanismCentered on the Speech Launch Threshold, this module makes otherwise opaque preverbal processing explicit and organizes it into operable steps, helping learners initiate and sustain more fluent L2 speech under real-time pressure.
Research Journey
2008–2010

Systematic Formal Knowledge and the Problem of Real-Time Generation

Between 2008 and 2010, systematic work on American pronunciation produced an integrated training system, while sustained study of traditional grammar, psycholinguistics, and philosophy of language yielded an explanatory framework for English syntax. Yet extensive formal knowledge and practice did not translate naturally into fluent speech, establishing the enduring question of how declarative linguistic knowledge becomes usable in real time.

2011–2013

Functional Expansion of the Syntax Framework and the Pursuit of Fluency

During M.A. study at Nankai University, continued American-pronunciation training and teaching were accompanied by the integration of systemic functional linguistics into the established syntax framework, with the aim of promoting fluency through more systematic and functionally organized sentence generation; the continuing difficulty of real-time production showed that syntactic refinement alone could not resolve the problem.

2014–2016

Pedagogical Translation of the Pronunciation and Syntax Systems

Following the appointment at Tianjin University of Finance and Economics Pearl River College in 2014, the established pronunciation and syntax systems were translated into explicit instructional practice. Their presentation won first prize in the 2015 FLTRP “Star Teacher” National Final; a subsequent account of American English sound-change patterns won first prize in the 2016 Second China Foreign Language Micro-Lecture Competition National Final.

2017–2020

The Persistent Fluency Problem and the Turn to Attentional Resources

Continued difficulty in sustaining spontaneous speech despite substantial linguistic knowledge exposed the divide between knowledge availability and online usability, gradually reframing the problem in terms of attentional limits, monitoring load, working memory, and real-time processing pressure.

2021

Attentional-Resource Expansion Strategies

Drawing on Segalowitz’s cognitive account of L2 fluency, an institutional key project on speech-rate reduction and pause extension developed temporal-support strategies intended to release attentional resources and create conditions for conceptual organization and linguistic encoding during online production.

2022

Initial Construction of the Operable Online Production Mechanism

A Tianjin Municipal Education Commission project shifted the inquiry from how processing time might be secured to how speech is organized within that time. Macroplan decomposition and linearization, preverbal organization in microplanning, and the Speech-Onset Timing Hypothesis together established an initial operable pathway from communicative intention to speech initiation.

2023

Theoretical Development Inside the Conceptualizer

The inquiry then moved inside the Conceptualizer through an integrated examination of cognitive science, conceptual semantics, cognitive grammar, working memory, and L2 speech-production models, extending the mechanism’s account of how communicative intention is formed and declarative knowledge converges into preverbal information.

2024

Formal Model Construction and Intelligent Applications

The twelfth China Foreign Language Education Fund project advanced the mechanism from a theoretical pathway toward formal model construction; in parallel, ChatGPT voice interaction supported the development of intelligent speaking assistants and pedagogical agents that embed the mechanism in spoken practice, immediate feedback, and instructional design.

2025

Reciprocal Integration of Research and Teaching

Publication of the operable online-production mechanism gave the framework a systematic scholarly account. In the same year, How to Speak English Fluently?, a teaching presentation centered on the mechanism, received the National Grand Prize in the Tenth National College English Teachers’ Basic Teaching Skills Competition, followed by first prize in the Tianjin Final of the Sixteenth SFLEP National Foreign Language Teaching Contest.

2026.07

The Speech Launch Threshold and International Scholarly Presentation

The paper Online Cognitive Processing in L2 Speech Production: An Operable Mechanism from Communicative Intention to Articulation Onset was presented at Beijing Foreign Studies University during the Inaugural Conference of the Division of Biolinguistics, Chinese Society for Cognitive Science, and the Fifth International Conference on Biolinguistics and Language Acquisition. The research isolated the Speech Launch Threshold and specified functional orientation, conceptual focus, and minimal relational configuration as the core preverbal conditions for speech initiation and continued progression.

2026.08

Building a Personal Academic Knowledge Infrastructure

The LiPei Research website and the Grand Map of Modern Linguistic Research were developed to organize traditional disciplines, theoretical frameworks, research methods, and emerging fields through four fundamental questions: constitution, dynamics, explanation, and inquiry and practice. This work brings previously dispersed research, theoretical reflection, and methodological resources into a searchable and expandable academic knowledge system, while locating the operable mechanism of online speech production within a broader landscape of language science.
