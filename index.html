About

Li Pei is an Associate Professor of English based in Tianjin, China. His work focuses on English speaking fluency, online speech production, pronunciation training, sentence construction, and operable approaches to spoken English development.

Research Focus

American Pronunciation

Precision in English sounds, articulatory awareness, and phonetic accuracy.

Speech Flow

Rhythm, stress, linking, reduction, and natural connected speech.

Sentence Construction

How thought becomes structure during real-time English speaking.

Fluency Mechanism

Investigating how speakers transform knowledge into fluent spoken output.

Featured Research

Operable Mechanism of English Speaking Online Production

An ongoing research framework exploring how speakers move from intention to fluent output under real-time cognitive pressure, with a focus on practical and teachable mechanisms.

Teaching & Projects

Current work integrates research on spoken English production with classroom teaching, micro-lesson design, pronunciation training, and English speaking development.
